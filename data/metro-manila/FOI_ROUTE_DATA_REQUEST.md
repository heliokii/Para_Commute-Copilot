# Draft only — LTFRB NCR route data request

**Do not submit without the user's authorization.** The FOI portal advisory checked 2026-10-09 reported that new-request actions were temporarily disabled. This is a draft for a later authorized submission.

## Subject

Request for current NCR public utility vehicle route inventory, service status, and data reuse terms

## Request

Good day. I am developing an offline public-transport route planner for passengers travelling within the National Capital Region. Please provide the latest available route and service inventory for traditional PUJ, modernized PUJ, UV Express, and city bus services operating in NCR.

For each route or direction, please include where available: route/franchise ID; mode and vehicle class; public route name; active, modified, suspended, or discontinued status and the effective date of that status; authorized origin, destination, and intermediate terminals/stops; authorized alignment or ordered waypoints; service days/hours or headway; authorized fleet/units; latest route circular or approval reference; and the date the dataset was last updated.

If held electronically, GeoJSON, Shapefile, KML, GTFS, or CSV is preferred. Please identify the applicable license, attribution, redistribution, and derivative-work conditions for incorporating the data into an offline passenger information application. If only part of the requested information is held, please release the available records and identify the remaining gaps. I am not requesting personal information.

Thank you.
