## Route source decision

Additional official route-list leads were checked on 2026-10-09. LTFRB's **Metro Manila End of SCP 3 Operations – List of Routes** PDF enumerates 84 route names, but it is a 2022 service-contracting program list and does not establish which services currently operate, stop locations, alignments, or travel times: [LTFRB list](https://ltfrb.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Metro-Manila-End-of-SCP-3-Operations_List-of-Routes.pdf). LTFRB's 2024 Resolution 053 also names PUJ/UVE routes with low unit counts, but a low-unit list is not an active route inventory or proof of current service: [Resolution 053](https://ltfrb.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/RESO-053-S-2024-1.pdf). Both are retained as research leads only.

MMDA's current [Love Bus notice](https://pia.gov.ph/news/first-lady-leads-inauguration-of-mmdas-revived-love-bus-interactive-gallery-annex-building/), published 2026-09-10, confirms the free C5 service began that day, runs 6 a.m.–9 p.m., and lists the road sequence from Robinsons Galleria to Eastwood City and back. It says all stops have waiting sheds. The notice does not provide exact stop coordinates, per-segment travel times, or a machine-readable route geometry, so this is not yet pack-ready. PIA states its content is public domain unless otherwise stated; the source page credits the operational details to MMDA.

Quezon City's [Q City Bus route guide](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/qcitizen-guides/quezon-city-bus-service-program/) was last updated 2026-08-04 and lists eight operating free-bus routes (daily except holidays). The city's [Route 6 stop advisory](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/q-city-bus-service-advisory-new-bus-stop-on-route-6/) gives 14 named stops and says the newest Gilmore Avenue stop took effect 2026-08-03; the city separately confirmed eight electric buses retained the route and schedule in May 2026. This is strong service/stop evidence for one route and an official route-map lead for the other seven, but the public materials do not provide stop coordinates or per-segment ride times in text. The source page offers route map/schedule images and names the TTMD contact, so the next usable step is extracting those current schedules/maps and obtaining segment times without substituting route headways for travel time.

The page content is CC BY-SA 2.0, but no routes were copied into the pack.

### Boundary candidate recheck, 2026-10-09

The Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX) API lists the Philippines COD-AB dataset under CC BY-IGO, but its metadata says the underlying boundaries were created on 2018-02-09, were considered valid for humanitarian use on 2018-03-26, and were reviewed for accuracy/completeness on 2024-04-01. Its 2026 metadata/resource timestamps are not boundary-vintage dates: [HDX Philippines COD-AB record](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-phl). The 2023 GitHub ADM1 copy says its raw shapefiles came from this OCHA dataset and were cleaned/reconciled to PSGC updates. That can update names/codes and reflect listed mergers, but does not establish a current NCR boundary; the GitHub repo's MIT code license also does not by itself resolve the underlying data terms: [2023 shapefile metadata](https://github.com/altcoder/philippines-psgc-shapefiles/blob/main/data/2023/Regions/phl_admbnda_adm1_psa_namria_20231106.shp.xml), [repository source notes](https://github.com/altcoder/philippines-psgc-shapefiles).

Decision: reject both as current-boundary certification. The HDX source is useful as a historical comparison and has a clear dataset license, but the 2018 geometry vintage is too old for strict present-day route clipping without an authoritative confirmation that NCR's geometry is unchanged. The 2023 repackaging does not remove that uncertainty.

## Rail fare pack (added 2026-10-10)

`node scripts/rail-fares-to-csv.mjs` turns `lrt1-fare-matrices.json`, `lrt2-fare-matrices.json` and `mrt3-fare-matrices.json` into pack CSVs in `rail-pack/` (2,136 fare cells, 51 stations, fares only, no routes). Station coordinates in `rail-station-coordinates.csv` come from OpenStreetMap via Overpass, captured 2026-10-10. Each row names its OSM element. OSM data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: attribute it in About if the pack ever ships. Coordinates are the mapped station building or platform, not surveyed. `rail-pack` still fails `validate:pack` on the missing `ncr_boundary.geojson`; see the boundary notes above. The fare products keep their `status` text as `conflict_note`: recheck discount products and reuse terms before activating. MRT-3's regular matrix states no effective date, so the capture date is used.
